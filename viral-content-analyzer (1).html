Ye Tool Kya Karta Hai

Viral Content Analyzer aapke caption, title, hook ya script ko 8 alag-alag factors par judge karta hai aur ek Viral Score (0-100) deta hai. Ye score batata hai ki aapka content engagement aur reach ke liye kitna optimized hai.

Step-by-Step Use

  • Platform chuno — YouTube, Instagram, Twitter, LinkedIn, Facebook ya Threads, kyunki har platform ke rules alag hain.
  • Content Type select karo — Caption, Title, Hook, Script ya Thread.
  • Apna content paste karo aur "Viral Score Nikalo" dabao.
  • Report padho — score, breakdown aur tips ke hisaab se apna content improve karo.
  • Agar aapke paas Claude API key hai, to "AI Se Deep Analysis" button se AI ek rewrite suggestion bhi dega.

Freelancers/Creators Ke Liye Monetization Ideas

  • Is tool ko apni Fiverr/Upwork gig mein bonus ke roop mein offer karo — "Free Viral Score Report" clients ko attract karega.
  • Content agencies ke liye white-label karke bech sakte ho — apna logo laga kar clients ko dedicated tool do.
  • Is tool ka link apne Instagram bio mein daal kar lead magnet bana sakte ho — jo bhi use karega, uska email collect karo aur baad mein apni paid service pitch karo.
  • YouTube par "Is Tool Se Maine Apna Reel Viral Kiya" jaisa video banao aur affiliate/consulting offer promote karo.

Score Kaise Samjhein

  • 80-100: Excellent — content viral hone ke strong chances hain.
  • 60-79: Good — thoda improvement se aur better ho sakta hai.
  • 40-59: Average — hook, CTA ya emotional trigger add karo.
  • 0-39: Weak — content ko restructure karna padega.
